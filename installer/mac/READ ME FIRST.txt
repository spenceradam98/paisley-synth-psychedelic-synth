INSTALLING PAISLEY ON A MAC

1. Right-click (or Ctrl-click) "Install Paisley.pkg" and choose Open.
   (Paisley isn't notarised by Apple yet, so a plain double-click shows a warning.
    If there's no Open button: System Preferences > Security & Privacy > General > "Open Anyway".)
2. Click through the installer.
3. Restart your DAW. Paisley is under Instruments (Logic: AU Instruments > Paisley Audio).

Works on macOS 10.13 or newer, Intel and Apple Silicon.
